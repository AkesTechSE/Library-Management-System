# Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our Library Management System.

## Information We Collect
- **Personal Information:** Name, email address, and user role (student, staff, admin).
- **Usage Data:** Borrowed books, reading activity, and system interactions.

## How We Use Your Information
- To provide and maintain the library services.
- To manage user accounts and permissions.
- To improve our services and user experience.

## Information Sharing
- We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties except as required by law or to provide core services (e.g., authentication, analytics).

## Data Security
- We use industry-standard security measures to protect your data.

## Your Rights
- You may request to access, update, or delete your personal information at any time.

## Contact Us
For questions about this policy, contact the library administrator.
