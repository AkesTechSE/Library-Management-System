# Service Details

The Library Management System provides the following services:

- Online catalog of books for browsing and searching.
- Borrowing and returning books with due date tracking.
- Personalized dashboard for students, staff, and admins.
- Secure authentication and user management.
- Analytics and reporting for library usage (admin only).

## Support
For support or questions, contact the library administrator.

## Service Availability
We strive to maintain high availability, but occasional downtime may occur for maintenance or upgrades.

## Feedback
We welcome feedback to improve our services. Please reach out to the admin with suggestions.
